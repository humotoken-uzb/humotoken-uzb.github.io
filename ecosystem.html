Asterium Ecosystem Map & Liquidity Routing

The Asterium network consists of 6 primary tokens, interconnected via central liquidity hubs $ONE and $TAXI. All assets are built on the Solana blockchain using the Token-2022 standard, with liquidity routed through Meteora Damm V2.
